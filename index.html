About S.L. Express

Pioneers in Indian Courier Since 1982.

We take pleasure in introducing SL Express as one of the pioneers in the Indian courier industry. Since the inception of our company in 1982, we have been steadily expanding our network to cover the far-flung areas of the country.

At present we have 4 Regional Offices, 58 major branch offices and 194 business associates to cater to the expanding requirements of esteemed organisations, complemented by exceptional speed, reliability and safety.

Domestic services apart, we are also fully geared to handle international consignments.
